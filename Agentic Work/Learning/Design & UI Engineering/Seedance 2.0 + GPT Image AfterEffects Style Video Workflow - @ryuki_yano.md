## Key Points

- Combines Seedance 2.0 with GPT Image 2.0 for AfterEffects-like motion graphics
- Produces 15-second, 16:9 cinematic Tokyo cityscape videos
- Features animated 2D ribbon trails, confetti, and hand-drawn sketch overlays on real footage
- Detailed 12-cut storyboard structure covering diverse Tokyo locations (Skytree, torii gates, crossings, train crossings, cherry blossom riverside, evening waterfront)
- Emphasizes natural integration of 2D effects with live-action depth and perspective

### Prompt Structure (Japanese)

The prompt specifies:
1. **Visual direction**: Real Tokyo cityscapes with overlaid colorful ribbon effects, confetti, and white sketch lines
2. **12 cut breakdown**: Each cut has specific location, camera movement, and effect layering
3. **Effect rules**: Pink, light blue, yellow, white palette; effects follow scene depth naturally
4. **Prohibitions**: No slideshow-style static images, no text/watermarks, no distortion artifacts

### Notable Techniques

- Effects integrated with scene perspective (not flat overlays)
- Camera movements: low-angle steadicam, dutch-angle, insert close-ups, overhead shots
- Transitions via effect flow and sky color matching between cuts
- Final cut emphasizes poetic, lingering sunset atmosphere
