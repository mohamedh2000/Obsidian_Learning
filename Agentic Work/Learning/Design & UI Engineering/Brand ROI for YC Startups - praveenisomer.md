## Key Points

- Worked with 12+ YC companies in 5 months (Hex, Autumn, Ishiki/Fern, Zest, Port Financial, Gojiberry)
- Brand and design systems that convert: one client closed $60K deal in first week, before YC funds arrived
- Process: audit existing positioning → analyze competitors → create strategic brand placement (enterprise vs consumer)
- Most founders treat brand as "nice to have" — the ones closing pre-revenue deals know brand is the ROI
