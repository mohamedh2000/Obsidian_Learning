## Key Points
- **Curated Website Gallery**: Collection of 1,000+ handpicked websites across industries — agencies, SaaS products, portfolios, e-commerce — representing best-in-class web design
- **Searchable by Attributes**: Filter by styles, fonts, frameworks, platforms, and technologies — enables targeted browsing for specific design approaches
- **Visual Previews**: Thumbnails and video previews of featured websites — see the design in motion before clicking through
- **Engagement Metrics**: View counts surface popular designs — helpful for identifying trends and crowd-validated excellence
- **Industry Coverage**: Spans design agencies (Lusion, Metalab) to SaaS products (Notion, Height) to personal portfolios — comprehensive cross-section of the web

### Why This Matters
When starting a new landing page, portfolio, or product site, Godly provides a curated starting point for design direction. Rather than browsing random sites hoping for inspiration, you get pre-filtered excellence across multiple design philosophies and industries.

### Use Cases
- **Project Kickoff**: Browse similar site categories before defining visual direction
- **Client Presentations**: Reference specific Godly entries to communicate design vision
- **Design System Research**: Study how different sites handle navigation, typography, spacing, and motion
- **Technology Scouting**: Filter by frameworks/platforms to see what's possible with specific tech stacks
- **Trend Tracking**: Regular browsing reveals emerging patterns in web design
