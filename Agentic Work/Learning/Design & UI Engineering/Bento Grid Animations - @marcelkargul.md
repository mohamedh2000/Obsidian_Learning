### Bento Animation Patterns
Common animation approaches for bento grids include:
- Staggered entrance animations (cards fade/slide in sequence)
- Hover-triggered depth shifts (parallax, scale, shadow)
- Scroll-linked reveal animations
- Micro-interactions within individual grid cells
- Background gradients or blob animations in cards
