## Key Points

- Combining dither motion effects with Japanese typography
- Goal: create more distinct, memorable website sections
- Dithering adds retro/textured feel to modern motion graphics
- Japanese typography brings unique visual character and cultural depth

## Design Pattern Context

Dithering is a technique that adds noise/texture patterns to gradients or solid colors, creating a lo-fi or retro aesthetic. When combined with Japanese typography (which has strong vertical orientation options and distinctive character shapes), it creates a visually striking contrast between analog texture and precise letterforms.
