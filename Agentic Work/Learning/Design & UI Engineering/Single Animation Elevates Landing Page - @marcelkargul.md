## Key Points
- **One animation as differentiator**: A well-chosen single animation can be the difference between forgettable and memorable — not quantity, but strategic placement
- **Hierarchy of polish**: "Decent" implies functional and well-designed; "elite" implies the extra craft layer that creates emotional response and brand recall
- **Diminishing returns awareness**: The framing "a single animation" suggests restraint — over-animating dilutes impact
- **High engagement validates insight**: 865 likes from design community indicates this resonates as practical wisdom, not just opinion

### Why Single Animations Work
The most effective landing page animations are:
- **Hero section entrance**: Logo/headline reveal that sets the tone
- **Scroll-triggered reveal**: Key value prop that demands attention at the right moment
- **CTA interaction**: Button hover/click state that rewards engagement
- **Background ambient motion**: Subtle gradients or particles that add depth without distraction

### Design Philosophy
This tweet captures a core principle: restraint creates impact. Amateur designers add animations everywhere; experienced designers choose *the* animation that matters most.
