## Key Points
- **AI acceleration raises the design bar**: With AI speeding up development velocity, the baseline expectation for UI polish has shifted upward
- **"Glow-up" as minimum standard**: Polishing common components (buttons, inputs, cards) should no longer be considered optional or premium work
- **Design engineering demand surge**: The market is recognizing designers who can implement—not just spec—high-quality interactions
- **Mundane components matter most**: The most-used UI primitives (inputs, dropdowns, modals) have the highest cumulative impact on perceived quality

### Why This Matters
The tweet captures a market shift: AI tools compress implementation time, so the differentiator becomes craft quality. Design engineers—hybrid roles who design AND build—are uniquely positioned to deliver that quality without handoff friction. This validates investment in learning animation, micro-interactions, and implementation skills alongside visual design.
