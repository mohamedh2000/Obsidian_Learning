## Key Points
- **Strong brand vs weak positioning**: ElevenLabs has excellent visual identity but buries conversion messaging
- **Homepage structure critique**: Key selling points placed 9 scrolls down instead of above the fold
- **"Bringing technology to life"**: Generic tagline vs actual product differentiation
- **Thread format**: Full teardown with actionable positioning lessons

### Design Lesson
Even with strong brand aesthetics, homepage information architecture matters — conversion-focused messaging should appear before the fold, not at the footer.
