> Fluid transitions should get snappier as users repeat them, using a stored interaction count to shorten duration and distance.

## Key Points

- Fluid transitions can feel better in design review than in real repeated use.
- Store a `transitionCount` cookie and scale transition duration and distance by `x / transitionCount`.
- The pattern makes repeated transitions progressively faster, reducing friction for returning users.
