## Key Points
- **detail.design**: A curated reference site documenting small design decisions that shape better interfaces
- **Curator**: Rene Wang (@renedotwang)—recognized for documenting micro-interaction patterns
- **"Tiny details, big difference"**: The site's premise is that craft at the micro level compounds into perceived quality
- **Endorsement signal**: Adham Dannaway (well-known design educator) recommending it validates the resource's quality

### What detail.design Covers
Based on the linked details in this batch:
- **Interruptible Animation**: Allowing close events without waiting for animations to complete
- **Animated Action Button**: Motion feedback on button interactions
- **Morphing Button to Input**: Shape-shifting transitions between UI states

### Why This Matters
Micro-interaction references are hard to find in one place. Most design inspiration sites focus on full-page layouts or component libraries, not the atomic-level decisions that create "feel." detail.design fills that gap with video-first documentation of specific patterns.
