## Key Points

- Use pure white background to make colorful typography pop
- Make hero text oversized and multicolored per word
- Float product keywords like YERBA and MATE around the headline
- Keep layout brutally minimal - text does all the work
- Add subtle cursor interactions to make it feel alive
- Let product colors tell the energy story visually
