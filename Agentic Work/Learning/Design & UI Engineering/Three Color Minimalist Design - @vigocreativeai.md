## Key Points
- Demonstrates that constraint breeds creativity: limiting to 3 colors (orange, black, white) forces stronger compositional decisions
- Eliminates crutches like gradients, shadows, and transparency that can mask weak design fundamentals
- Shows that visual "flavor" (味道) comes from intentional restraint, not from having more options
- Part of the minimalist design philosophy that values doing more with less

### Why This Matters
Design constraints often produce stronger outcomes than unlimited palettes. By removing gradients, shadows, and transparency, designers must rely on composition, contrast, and hierarchy alone. The orange-black-white combination provides high contrast while maintaining warmth—a lesson applicable to UI systems, branding, and editorial design.
