### Interaction System Design Philosophy
The focus on making it "feel like a precise visual mechanism" suggests:
- **Predictable feedback**: Every action has a clear, immediate response
- **Mechanical metaphors**: Interactions that feel like physical controls (dials, levers, toggles)
- **Timing precision**: Easing curves and durations that feel "right" rather than arbitrary
- **Sound-motion sync**: Audio cues that reinforce the visual feedback loop

This demonstrates Claude Code's utility beyond traditional development:
- Rapid prototyping of interaction concepts
- Quick iteration on micro-interaction timing
