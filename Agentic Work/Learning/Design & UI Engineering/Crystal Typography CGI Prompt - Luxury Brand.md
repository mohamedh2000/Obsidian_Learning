## Key Points

- **Phase 0 - Brand Intelligence**: Retrieve exact canonical logotype from training data, trademark-accurate
- **Phase 1 - Crystal Material**: Optical borosilicate glass, IOR 1.85-2.1, maximum dispersion
  - Wear system: micro-scratches, edge chips, surface haze zones, fingerprint ghosts
- **Phase 2 - Light & Dispersion**: Single collimated light at 35°, full chromatic dispersion following Snell's law
  - Edge glow with total internal reflection, chromatic aberration
- **Phase 3 - Background**: Pure absolute black (#000000-#050505), letters as sole light source
- **Phase 4 - Composition**: 3/4 angle (5-10° rotation), 60-70% frame width, 1:1 or 4:5 aspect
- **Phase 5 - Tech Specs**: Octane Render, 16+ bounces, 2048 samples/pixel, physically accurate wavelength-dependent IOR
