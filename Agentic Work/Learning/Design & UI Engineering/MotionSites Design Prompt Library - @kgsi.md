## Key Points

- MotionSites offers named design prompts and templates that can be pasted into AI site builders such as Bolt.new.
- The collection reportedly includes more than 50 hero sections, more than 20 landing pages, footers, and animated backgrounds.
- The tweet frames the value as consistency: starting from named examples produces a more predictable direction than asking an AI builder for a generic polished hero.
- The author notes that many examples are visually distinctive, so they are better used as starting references for adaptation than copied directly.
