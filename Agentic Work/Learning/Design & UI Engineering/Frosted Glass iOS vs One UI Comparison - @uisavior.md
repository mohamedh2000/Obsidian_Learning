## Key Points
- **iOS (Clear):** Maintains crisp, vibrant backdrop where colors pop through cleanly—preserves depth and visual interest behind the blur layer
- **One UI (Blur):** Uses heavy blur that kills detail and vibrancy, resulting in a "muddy" appearance that flattens the visual hierarchy
- The core principle: frosted glass effects should feel lightweight and premium, not obscure the content behind them
- **Less blur = more elegance:** The amount of blur is inversely proportional to perceived quality—subtle effects outperform aggressive ones

### Design Implications
When implementing frosted glass / glassmorphism:
1. Use blur radius sparingly (iOS uses ~20-30px vs One UI's ~60-80px)
2. Preserve color saturation in the blurred layer
3. Add subtle luminosity to maintain depth perception
4. Test on vibrant backgrounds, not just solid colors
