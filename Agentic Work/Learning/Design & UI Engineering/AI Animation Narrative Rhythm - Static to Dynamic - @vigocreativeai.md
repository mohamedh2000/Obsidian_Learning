## Key Points

### The Animation Sequence Breakdown
1. **Camera Focus** — Start with old comic page flip establishing context
2. **Fourth Wall Break** — Character's eyes suddenly look at you
3. **2D to 3D Transition** — Character tears through paper, jumps out of the 2D plane
4. **Action Climax** — Gun raised, fire, frame explodes

### Narrative Rhythm Framework
- Not about making things "move" — about making AI understand **narrative pacing**
- Three-beat structure: **蓄力 (buildup) → 突破 (breakthrough) → 释放 (release)**
- One static image + one prompt can go from still → motion → explosion

### Prompt Engineering Insight
- AI animation succeeds when prompts encode **storytelling beats**, not just motion descriptors
- The fourth-wall break technique creates dramatic tension through viewer engagement
- Dimensional shift (2D paper → 3D space) amplifies impact of the climax
