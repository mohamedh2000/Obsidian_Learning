## Key Points
- **Onboarding as animation showcase**: First-run experiences are prime real estate for motion design — users are attentive, expectations are forming
- **Micro-interactions reduce cognitive load**: Small animated feedback (progress indicators, success states, input validation) guide users through unfamiliar flows
- **Emotional design during setup**: Delightful animations during onboarding create positive first impressions that influence long-term retention
- **High engagement (768 likes)**: Design community values onboarding-specific animation examples as they're high-impact but under-documented

### Critical Onboarding Micro-interactions
1. **Progress indicators**: Stepped progress bars with smooth fills, dot transitions
2. **Input feedback**: Shake on error, checkmark on valid, character counters
3. **Page transitions**: Directional slides that reinforce forward momentum
4. **Success celebrations**: Confetti, checkmarks, subtle scale bounces on completion
5. **Loading states**: Skeleton screens, shimmer effects during data fetch
6. **Tooltip choreography**: Staggered feature callouts with pointer animations

### Why Onboarding Matters for Motion
Users encountering an app for the first time are:
- Forming mental models of how the app "feels"
- More tolerant of decorative animation (less task-focused)
- Building trust through perceived polish
- Encoding brand personality through motion language
