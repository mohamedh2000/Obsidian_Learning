## Key Points
- Clickable dots on slide sidebar serve as both position indicator and table of contents navigation
- Hover interaction reveals slide title — progressive disclosure without cluttering the view
- Key insight: treating slides as websites unlocks web-native interactive capabilities (hover states, click handlers, animations, dynamic content)
- Contrast with traditional slide decks: PowerPoint/Keynote are static; HTML slides can be living documents

### Why It Matters
Challenges the "slides are static" assumption. Modern web capabilities (CSS hover, JS interactions, responsive design) turn presentations into interactive experiences. Relevant for:
- Conference talks where audience can explore at their own pace
- Documentation that combines narrative with interactivity
- Product demos where users can click through features
