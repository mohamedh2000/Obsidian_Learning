> Ascii Magic adds a large dither system with 15 algorithms, 17 palettes, tunable controls, color-channel modes, and animated matrix effects.

## Key Points

- Adds 15 dithering algorithms including Bayer grids, Floyd-Steinberg, Atkinson, Blue Noise, Halftone, Lines, and Radial.
- Ships with 17 palettes such as Game Boy, CGA, Pico-8, C64, Risograph, and Sepia, plus custom palette support.
- Exposes detailed visual controls for pixel size, strength, contrast, threshold, cell size, and angle.
- Supports chroma modes for standard color, luminance-only, and per-channel RGB processing.
- Includes animated matrix effects with eight directions, shimmer, and speed control.
