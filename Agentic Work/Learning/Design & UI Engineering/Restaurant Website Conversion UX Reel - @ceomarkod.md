## Key Points

- Frames a $3K restaurant website as a business asset, not just a polished visual build.
- Emphasizes fast loading because visitors abandon slow sites quickly.
- Calls out menu and booking access as primary actions that should be immediately visible.
- Prioritizes mobile execution because most restaurant discovery traffic happens on phones.
- Community replies push back on excessive scroll-heavy design, making the reel useful as a conversion-versus-spectacle reference.
