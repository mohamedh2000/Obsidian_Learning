## Key Points

- 91% of designers now use AI weekly (up from 54% in 2025)
- Average designer uses 7 AI tools
- Claude (78%) overtook ChatGPT (65%) in designer adoption
- Creating micro-tools is a mega-trend
- 62% cite inconsistent quality as #1 challenge
- 80% rely on their own taste for final visuals
- Peer learning exploded from 24% to 70%
- 60% of leaders plan to keep or grow headcount
- Companies prioritize AI fluency, systems thinking, and strategy over pixel perfection
- Designers who code & prototype feel 2x more creative and capable
