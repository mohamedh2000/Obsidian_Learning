## Key Points

- Most AI brands follow the same visual language: gradients, geometric patterns, safe sans-serifs, polished-but-generic systems
- Differentiation starts with the question: "What makes this company different, and what visual language honestly reflects that?"
- ParchAI: calm, warm, almost editorial feel
- SigmaAI: rigorous and human, built for founders who value trust over hype
- MetricAI: bold, colourful, confident — a data brand that doesn't feel cold
- Different companies should feel different — that's the whole point of brand design
