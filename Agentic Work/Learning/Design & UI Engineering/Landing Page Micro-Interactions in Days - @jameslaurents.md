### Micro-Interaction Categories
Typical landing page micro-interactions include:
- **Hover states**: Button color shifts, icon animations, card elevations
- **Scroll-triggered reveals**: Fade-in, slide-up, parallax effects
- **Input feedback**: Form field focus animations, validation states
- **Navigation transitions**: Menu open/close, page transitions
- **Loading states**: Skeleton screens, progress indicators

### Design Velocity Signal
This tweet demonstrates that polished micro-interactions don't require weeks of iteration. With established patterns and component libraries, "obsessive detail" can be achieved rapidly when the designer knows what works.
