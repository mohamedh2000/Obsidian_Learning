# Time Series Database Deep Dive

> Time-series databases are built for timestamped measurements, retention, and trend queries, not everyday CRUD.

Local HTML diagram covering time-series database architecture and applications.

**Local file:** `~/.agent/diagrams/time-series-database-deep-dive.html`

## Key Points
- Time-series systems optimize for high-volume timestamped writes, windows, rollups, compression, and retention policies.
- Cardinality and downsampling are central design concerns because raw measurements accumulate faster than teams want to store forever.
- Best fit is telemetry, monitoring, anomaly detection, and historical metrics, not replacing transactional product tables.
