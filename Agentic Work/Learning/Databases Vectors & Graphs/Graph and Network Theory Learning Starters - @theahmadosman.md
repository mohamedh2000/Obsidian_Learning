## Key Points

- Graph and network theory are positioned as high-ROI foundations across distributed systems, AI, infrastructure, markets, and social dynamics.
- Suggested books include Barabasi's Network Science, Easley and Kleinberg's Networks, Crowds, and Markets, Diestel's Graph Theory, and Kleinberg and Tardos's Algorithm Design.
- Suggested courses and tools include MIT Math for CS, Spielman's Spectral Graph Theory, Roughgarden's Algorithmic Game Theory, CS224W, NetworkX, Gephi, SNAP, and PyG.
