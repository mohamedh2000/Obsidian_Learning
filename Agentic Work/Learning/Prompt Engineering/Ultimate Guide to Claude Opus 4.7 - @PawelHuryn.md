## Key Points

- Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.7 on April 16 — it "takes instructions literally" and "will not silently generalize"
- Boris Cherny (Claude Code lead): "It took a few days for me to learn how to work with it effectively"
- **The Universal Unlock**: 4.7 rewards clear intent — not longer prompts, not more rules, not bigger CLAUDE.md
- **Strategic context** is durable: what you're building, who it's for, what's off-limits (write once in CLAUDE.md)
- **Per-task intent** is variable: what specifically do I want Claude to do right now
- Approach shift: from imperative to declarative — give success criteria, not step-by-step instructions
- Aligned with Karpathy's advice: "Don't tell it what to do, give it success criteria and watch it go"
