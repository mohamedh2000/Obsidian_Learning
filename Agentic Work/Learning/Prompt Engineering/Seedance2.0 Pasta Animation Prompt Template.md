## Key Points

- Each cut includes: angle, subject, lens direction, and ambient sound

## Prompt Structure

### Style Block
- Japanese full-color anime aesthetic
- Fast cuts, high frame count
- Cel-shaded shadows, cinematic cooking scene pacing
- Character hand + ingredient consistency emphasis

### Visual Style
- Movie-quality cooking footage
- Warm white indirect light + steam rim lighting
- Anamorphic lens flare, shallow DOF, 35mm film-like grain
- Teal & orange color grading

### Setting
- Bright home kitchen: white tiles, wooden cutting board, silver frying pan
- Natural daylight

### 10-Cut Sequence
1. Extreme close-up: knife slicing garlic rhythmically
2. Macro overhead: sliced garlic spread on cutting board (silence)
3. Low angle: olive oil poured into pan, backlit glow
4. Extreme close-up: garlic + chili dropped into oil (sizzle)
5. Macro side view: bubbles rising, garlic turning golden
6. Overhead: pasta swirling in boiling water, steam rising
7. Low angle at pan edge: pasta dropped in, explosive steam burst
8. Medium close-up diagonal: vigorous pan toss, pasta airborne
9. Bust shot: tongs plating pasta onto white dish
10. Overhead: parsley falling from fingers onto completed dish (silence)
