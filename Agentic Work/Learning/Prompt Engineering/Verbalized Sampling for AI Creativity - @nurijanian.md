# Verbalized Sampling for AI Creativity

> A training-free prompting technique that counteracts typicality bias - the tendency of aligned LLMs to converge on most probable outputs.

## Key Points

- **Typicality bias**: When you ask for 5 ideas, you get 1 idea sampled 5 times with slight variations
- **The technique**: Ask for a distribution instead of items, force model to assign probabilities, tell it to sample from the tails (unlikely region)
- **Result**: 1.6x to 2.1x increase in output diversity on creative/ideation tasks
- **Why alternatives fail**:
  - "Give me 5 ideas again" → samples from same head of distribution
  - Higher temperature → changes wording, not solution space
  - Regenerating → same mechanism, five samples from head
  - Persona-swap → produces stylistic variation, not structural diversity
