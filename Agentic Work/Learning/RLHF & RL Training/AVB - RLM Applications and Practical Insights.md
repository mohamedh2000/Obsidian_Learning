## Key Points

- Applied RLMs to multiple problem domains:
  - QA from production logs
  - Video understanding
  - Summarizing across multiple research papers while retaining citations
  - Analyzing textarena rollouts to extract distilled skills
- **Key finding #1**: Underlying model quality is the primary success factor
- **Key finding #2**: Environment-specific prompts, data structures, and guidelines outperform the general universal prompt from the RLM paper
- **Key finding #3**: Indexing information on repeated tasks for reuse helps significantly, especially in chat-based tasks (e.g., production logs)
- **Prediction**: Once big labs train models inside RLM harnesses, current meta will require quick redesign
