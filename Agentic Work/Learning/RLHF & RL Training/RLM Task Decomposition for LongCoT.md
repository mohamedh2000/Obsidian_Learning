# RLM Task Decomposition for LongCoT

> Training to decompose tasks could boost RLM performance on LongCoT benchmarks — with two eval settings: Python-assisted (avoiding pure reasoning) vs pure LLM/RLM reasoning.

## Key Points

- Evaluated untrained RLMs on LongCoT benchmark and found that training to decompose tasks would boost performance
- Two evaluation settings considered: (1) Python may be used to avoid reasoning (dotted bars) and (2) LLM/RLM does all the reasoning (solid bars)
