## Key Points

- **Market-based coordination**: Agents bid in auctions to win the right to take environment steps
- **Emergent reasoning**: Complex behaviors emerge from simple agents in multi-agent scenarios
- **External evolution**: The market simulator controls which agents evolve—agents don't know they're in a market
- **Optimized routing**: End result is specialized agents + intelligent routing mechanism
- **Not financial training**: This optimizes agents on verifiable environments (math, code, research)—not trading

## How It Works

1. Agents bid in auctions to take steps in target environments
2. Winning deficits their wallet; they take an action stepping environment forward
3. Future agents that continue successful trajectories accumulate wealth
4. Over time, this produces optimized population with specific instructions on when/how to act
