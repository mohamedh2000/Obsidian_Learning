## Key Points

- **Persistent sandbox infra** with filesystem & credential management
- **Skill file** with good priors & best practices for harness engineering & experiments
- **CLIs** for external info + managed SFT/RL training access
- **Externally enforced budget** with updates on remaining spend (agent cards?)
- **Harness primitive** like `/goal` to scope the problem

## Notes

A concise checklist for building auto-research systems. The insight is that frontier models can self-direct research if given:
1. Persistence (filesystem + credentials)
2. Domain knowledge (skill files)
3. Tool access (CLIs, training APIs)
4. Resource constraints (budget enforcement)
5. Goal framing (harness primitives)
