# Rising Agent Costs and Small Models

> The best way to understand data is often by reading it entirely — great for agents, expensive at scale. Open/small models + RL/finetuning can reduce costs by orders of magnitude.

## Key Points

- Often the best way to fully understand data is by reading the whole thing — pipe to a well-designed agent
- Gets incredibly expensive at scale with expensive models — every team contends with rising LLM costs
- Important use-case: mining signal from traces, transcripts, logs
- Heuristics help (formatting, filtering, model choice) but best perf is often letting agents "figure it out"
- Open and small models reduce costs by orders of magnitude without sacrificing perf
- RL and finetuning matter — with enough signal, fine-tune a small model to excel at the task
