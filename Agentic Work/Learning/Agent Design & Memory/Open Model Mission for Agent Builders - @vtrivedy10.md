## Key Points

- The age of the token subsidy is being pulled back — open models offer massive cost savings

- Instead of Haiku try DeepSeekv4 Flash, Nemotron
- Open models require tuning to work well in your harness for your task (why open harnesses matter)
- First use of open models is often as subagents or using closed frontier model as Advisor to open driver model
- LangChain leaning into open models heavily across products and deepagents
