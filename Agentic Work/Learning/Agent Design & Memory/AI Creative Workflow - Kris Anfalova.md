## Key Points

- **Ideas & Structure**: Gemini for brainstorming the entire process
- **Creative Generation**: Midjourney for base images + Nano Banana 2 for precise editing
- **Storytelling & Details**: OpenAI for expansion + Magnific AI for upscaling
- **Animation & Assembly**: Hailuo AI / Dream Machine for bringing to life, Figma + Jitter for finalization
