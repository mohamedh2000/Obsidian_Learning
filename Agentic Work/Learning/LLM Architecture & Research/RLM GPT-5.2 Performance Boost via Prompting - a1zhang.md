## Key Points
- Doubled best-performing score on LongCoT-mini (38.7% → 65.6%)
- No training required - pure prompting optimization
- Challenges LongCoT paper's attribution of failures to task decomposition
- "Mismanaged geniuses hypothesis" (MGH): capabilities exist, just poorly prompted
- RLM harness unlocks compositional reasoning in frontier models
- Builds on @raw_works's blogpost and Terminal-Bench work
- Implications for how we train/prompt reasoning systems
