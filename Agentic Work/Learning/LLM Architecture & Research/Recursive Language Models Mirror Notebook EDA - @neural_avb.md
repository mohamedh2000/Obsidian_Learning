## Key Points

- Recursive language model workflows can be understood as iterative notebook-style exploration over a persistent REPL.
- Small print and search operations let the model inspect slices of context without loading the full dataset into one prompt.
- Subagents or helper functions can transform narrow parts of the problem, and their returned variables can be composed into a final answer.
