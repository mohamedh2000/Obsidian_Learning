## Key Points

- **12 Free MIT Textbooks**: Graduate-level AI textbooks from researchers who built the field, all freely available
- **Six Curriculum Layers**: ML foundations, deep learning, reinforcement learning, algorithms, ethics, probability theory
- **Context vs Reference**: Uploading books as permanent context means Claude reasons FROM them, not just searches them
- **Behavior Shift**: Claude stopped pattern-matching and started explaining WHY signals exist
- **Example**: Claude pushed back on Kelly Criterion for crypto, citing fat-tailed distributions from the Probabilistic ML textbook

## The Key Insight

```
BEFORE: "RSI overbought, MA convergence" (pattern matching)
         │
         ▼
AFTER:  "Distribution properties deviate from statistical norms"
        (reasoning from probability theory)
```

## Practical Application

- Upload primary source material (textbooks, papers) into Claude Projects
- Use them as permanent context, not just reference material
- Expect Claude to reason from theoretical foundations, not just pattern-match
