# Mismanaged Geniuses Hypothesis

> Alex Zhang shares his "Mismanaged Geniuses Hypothesis": frontier LLMs are severely underutilized because of sub-optimal individual calls, and the next leap may come from training models to decompose and manage tasks rather than continued scaling.

## Key Points
