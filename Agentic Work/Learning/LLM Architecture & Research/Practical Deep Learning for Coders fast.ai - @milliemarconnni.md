## Key Points

- Jeremy Howard (twice the #1-ranked ML competitor) argues universities teach AI backwards — a year of linear algebra, calculus, and probability before you touch a model, which kills motivation at the "math wall."
- His analogy: you don't teach baseball by drilling curveball physics for a year before letting someone hold a bat — let them play, explain the physics once they care.
- The course inverts it: lesson one trains a real, running image classifier; theory is pulled in piece by piece exactly when needed to go deeper.
- HBR noted fast.ai can take motivated students to building industrial-grade AI systems. It's fully free, no paywall or signup tricks.
- Bar to entry: a little coding ability and some high-school math. Course link: https://course.fast.ai/
