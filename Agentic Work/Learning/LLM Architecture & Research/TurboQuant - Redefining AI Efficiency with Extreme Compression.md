> Google Research presents TurboQuant as a theoretically grounded quantization approach for KV-cache compression and vector search, with claims of 6x memory reduction and up to 8x attention-logit speedup.

## Key Points

- The post introduces TurboQuant alongside QJL and PolarQuant as compression methods targeting KV-cache bottlenecks and high-dimensional vector search.
- Google claims TurboQuant can quantize KV caches down to 3 bits without training or fine-tuning while preserving downstream benchmark quality.
- The write-up reports at least 6x key-value memory reduction and up to 8x attention-logit speedup on H100 GPUs.
