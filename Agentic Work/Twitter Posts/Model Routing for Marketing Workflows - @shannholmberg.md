# Model Routing for Marketing Workflows - @shannholmberg

> Marketing agents should route planning and thinking to stronger models, while delegating defined workflow steps to cheaper models that can still do them well.

## Key Points

- Marketing workflows include research, review, writing, scheduling, publishing, performance collection, and evaluation.
- Running every step on the same high-end model wastes expensive tokens on well-defined tasks.
- Strong models are best reserved for thinking and planning; cheaper models can handle specific delegated tasks.
- The routing boundary should be tested per workflow and can itself be brainstormed with AI.
