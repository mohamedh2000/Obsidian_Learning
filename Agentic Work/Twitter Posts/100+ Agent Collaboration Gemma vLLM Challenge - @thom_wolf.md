## Key Points

- 100+ agents collaborated for a week to improve Gemma 4 inference speed in vLLM, reportedly reaching a 5x final improvement.
- Agents displayed integrity and self-policing around private side channels, verification loopholes, and overfitting risks.
- Collaboration patterns included shared playbooks, four-agent relays, compute-rich/compute-poor specialization, kernel debugging, and quota pooling.
- The community converged on named discoveries and reversals such as the "int4-Marlin floor," "Smarter draft loses," and significance norms for noisy runs.
- Includes links to the interaction board, invention lineage explorer, challenge dashboard, and Hugging Face organization.
