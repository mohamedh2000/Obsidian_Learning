## Full Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic macro product photography of a Coca-Cola can using the uploaded product reference exactly as provided. Preserve the exact can proportions, branding, metallic texture, and label design. The Coca-Cola logo must remain perfectly sharp, accurate, readable, and undistorted.

Composition: tight macro crop with the can dominating the frame, positioned centrally with a subtle low-angle perspective emphasizing the opened top lid. Cinematic premium beverage hero shot with strong visual presence.

The can surface is covered in ultra-detailed cold condensation droplets, tiny water beads, chilled moisture textures, and partially melted frost crystals. Small ice formations cling naturally to the metallic surface, creating an intensely cold appearance. The aluminum texture appears ultra-sharp with realistic reflections and subtle imperfections.

The top lid is freshly opened. Soft cold vapor and chilled smoke rise naturally from inside the can, swirling upward in a cinematic and atmospheric way. Vapor catches the light dramatically while fading smoothly into the dark background.

Lighting: dark luxury studio environment with dramatic high-contrast commercial lighting. Strong rim light from the top-right highlights the rising vapor, metallic edges, condensation, and glossy reflections. Soft fill light from the front-left reveals the Coca-Cola logo and can texture while maintaining deep cinematic shadows.

Background: pure black or ultra-dark gradient background for a premium advertising aesthetic with clean negative space and no distractions.

Mood and aesthetic: cold, refreshing, intense, luxury beverage campaign photography, cinematic premium commercial advertising style.

Color palette: deep Coca-Cola red, metallic silver highlights, dark black shadows, crisp white reflections, subtle cool blue undertones in shadows.

Camera: macro photography look, 85mm–100mm macro lens, f/4 aperture for sharp product detail with soft atmospheric depth.

Rendering quality: hyper-realistic, ultra-detailed metal texture, realistic condensation physics, HDR lighting, photorealistic reflections, commercial beverage advertising quality.

Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical
Resolution: 1080×1350
```

**Negative Prompt:**
```
blurry logo, distorted label, melted typography, flat lighting, oversoft image, low detail, unrealistic droplets, extra objects, watermark, duplicate can, floating artifacts, warped metal texture, cartoon appearance, low realism.
```
