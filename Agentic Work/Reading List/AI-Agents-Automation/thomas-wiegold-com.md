# MiniMax M2.5 Review - Why I'm Seriously Considering Ditching Claude

> Thomas Wiegold argues that MiniMax M2.5 paired with OpenCode CLI gets close enough to frontier coding performance that Claude's price premium starts to look negotiable.

Hands-on review of MiniMax M2.5 as a coding model, focused on benchmark positioning, real-world workflow impressions, and the cost-performance tradeoff against Claude.

## Key Points

- The review claims M2.5 landed on February 12, 2026 and scored within 0.6% of Claude Opus 4.6 on SWE-Bench Verified while costing a fraction as much.
- It describes M2.5 as a 230B-parameter MoE model with 10B active parameters, open weights, a 204.8k-token context window, and up to 128k output tokens.
- A major part of the argument is workflow fit: Wiegold pairs the model with OpenCode CLI and says the model behaves with an architect-style planning bias before implementation.
