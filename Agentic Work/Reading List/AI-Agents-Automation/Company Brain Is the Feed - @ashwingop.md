## Key Points

- Social media feeds are effectively AI systems representing platform interests
- The feed watches, remembers signals, models behavior, and decides what each person sees
- Companies run internal attention systems through Slack, email, calendar, and escalation
- Coordination problems arise when context is fragmented across teams (SOC 2 example)
- Personal AI makes individuals louder but doesn't solve company-level coordination
- The feed's architecture: memory system + utility function + multiplayer environment
