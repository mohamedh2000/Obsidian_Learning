## Key Points
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**: Optimizing content for AI assistants and answer engines that surface direct responses
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**: Optimizing for LLM-generated search results (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews)
- Claims traditional pSEO (programmatic SEO) is becoming less relevant as AI-mediated discovery grows
- Links to seoitis.com/ai-suite as a tool for AI visibility optimization

### The Shift
Traditional SEO optimizes for Google's link-based ranking algorithm. AEO/GEO optimizes for how AI systems understand and cite content:
- Structured data and clear factual claims (easier for LLMs to extract)
- Authoritative sourcing (LLMs trained to prefer cited, reputable sources)
- Direct answer formatting (matches how AI assistants respond to queries)
