# LLMs Txt SEO for AI - @tomilola_ng

> To get your website seen on ChatGPT, Gemini, and the rest, do this: 1. Add a detailed summary about your product or services as an llms.txt file on your website 2. Then submit the page to these websites: llmstxt.site, directory.llmstxt.cloud 3. (Optional) You can also submit it to Google Search Console
