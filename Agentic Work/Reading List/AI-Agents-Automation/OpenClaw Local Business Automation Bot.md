## Key Points

- Scrapes indie businesses from Google Maps in real time
- Filters by review count, rating, and industry vertical
- Maps each vertical to the right product type
- Pulls logo and samples color palette from business's visual identity
- AI-renders photoreal mockup of logo on product
- Writes personalized postcard with owner's name and buy link
- Print-on-demand API auto-prints and ships when customer pays
