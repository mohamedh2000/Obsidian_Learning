## Key Points

1. **Deep internalization** — Actually thinking through every assumption and statement makes you better in that domain
2. **Faster workflow** — LLMs are "atrocious writers" and editing/reworking slop is long and exhausting
3. **Audience connection** — Writing for your audience instead of expecting people to read AI slop builds more genuine connections

## Notes

Counter-argument to the "let AI write everything" crowd. The thesis: human-written technical content is faster, deeper, and more relatable than LLM-edited content.
