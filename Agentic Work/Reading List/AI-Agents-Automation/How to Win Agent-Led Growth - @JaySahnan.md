## Key Points

- Browserbase reports doubled customer base, 119% self-serve revenue growth, and activation gains after focusing on agent-led growth.
- Agent-led growth splits into AEO, where agents discover and recommend the product, and AX, where agents can use it successfully.
- Programmatic use-case pages, broad public presence, and Reddit participation are treated as ways to become visible to agents.
- Agent experience means measuring agents as users, fixing docs and package-name friction, and designing onboarding paths for machine users.
- The article argues that agents are becoming a new top-of-funnel channel for GTM teams.

## Tweet

How to win Agent-led Growth.

In the last 3 months at Browserbase, we've doubled our customer base, grew our self-serve revenue by 119% and increased our activation by >10%, and it is not slowing down.

TL;DR Agents are changing how people discover and adopt tools. Winning means being the product the agent recommends and making onboarding frictionless for agents once they land.
