## Key Points

- MiroFish launches thousands of agents with different beliefs to debate and form coalitions
- Builds relationship graphs from uploaded events, producing scenario maps with probability estimates
- Uses expected value formula EV = p * W - (1-p) * L where p is scenario frequency across 1M simulations
