## Key Points

- Uses GPT Image 2 + Seedance 2.0 on TapNow_AI platform
- 9-panel storyboard structure with strict continuity requirements
- Emphasizes consistency: same cyclist identity, baby pink apparel, lighting continuity
- Premium commercial aesthetic (Nike/Rapha style) with hyper-realistic documentary look
- Detailed instructions for rain physics, camera movement, and cinematic lighting

## Prompt Breakdown

**Key continuity rules:**
- Follow storyboard strictly Panel 1-9, no skipping/merging
- SAME female cyclist identity throughout (face, helmet, body proportions)
- Baby pink dominant apparel color
- Consistent nighttime lighting (cool blue tones + red light reflections)
- Heavy rain intensity consistent across scenes
- Realistic water physics (droplets, splashes, mist, wheel spray)

**Cinematic requirements:**
- Real cinema rig camera movement (FPV drones, bike cameras, tracking)
- Subtle motion, no AI jitter
- Shallow depth of field in macro shots
- Dark, moody environment with high contrast wet reflections
