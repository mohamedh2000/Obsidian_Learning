## Key Points
- **llms.txt** is an emerging standard for making site content accessible to LLM-based systems (ChatGPT plugins, Perplexity, AI search)
- Unlike sitemap.xml which tells crawlers *where* content is, llms.txt tells AI systems *what* content means and how to use it
- The provocative framing ("more important than sitemap") suggests AI-driven discovery will overtake traditional search for certain queries
- Links to seoitis.com/ai-suite — likely a tool or guide for implementing AI-optimized SEO

### Why It Matters
```
Traditional SEO Path          AI SEO Path
────────────────────          ────────────────
sitemap.xml                   llms.txt
  ▼                              ▼
Google bot crawls             LLM agent reads
  ▼                              ▼
Indexed in search             Indexed in AI context
  ▼                              ▼
User Googles → clicks         User asks AI → gets answer
```

As AI assistants become primary information interfaces, content that's machine-readable for LLMs will capture attention that previously went through search engines.
