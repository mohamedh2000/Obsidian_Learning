# OpenClaw Restaurant Outreach Bot

> Automated system that finds restaurants with ugly food photos, redesigns them as Instagram posts, and mails the owner a postcard — full pipeline for social media agencies.

## Key Points

- Scrapes every restaurant in a city in real time
- Filters by review count, rating, last post date, photo quality
- Pulls strongest food photo from Google Maps reviews
- Samples brand palette from restaurant's visual identity
- AI-redrafts photo into 9:16 brand-matched Instagram post
- Writes postcard quoting real reviewer + dish
- Mails postcard to owner by first name with preview QR
- Full automation from discovery to brand-matching to outreach
