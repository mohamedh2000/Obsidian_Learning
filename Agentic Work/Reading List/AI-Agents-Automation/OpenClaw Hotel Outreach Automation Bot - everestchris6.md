## Key Points

### Full Automation Pipeline
1. **Scrapes** every hotel in a city in real time
2. **Filters** by review count + rating + last post date + photo quality
3. **Pulls** strongest interior shot from Google Maps listing
4. **Samples** brand palette from hotel's visual identity
5. **AI-redrafts** photo into 1:1 brand-matched Instagram post
6. **Writes** postcard quoting real reviewer + exact space
7. **Mails** hand-addressed postcard with preview QR to owner

### Use Case
- Social-media agencies can use this to land hotel clients
- Fully automated from discovery to brand-matching to outreach
