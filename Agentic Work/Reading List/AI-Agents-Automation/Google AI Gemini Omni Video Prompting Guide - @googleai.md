## Key Points
- **Tip 1: Leverage Real-World Knowledge** — Use cultural touchstones, historical eras, scientific terms directly; Gemini understands context
- **Tip 2: Take Control of Text Rendering** — Specify typography, placement, animation styles, and visual effects
- **Tip 3: Direct Your Camera Like a Pro** — Use cinematography terms: "push in", "dolly zoom", "locked off", "pan left"
- Available on Gemini App, Flow by Google, Google Flow Music, YouTube Shorts

## Example Prompts
- `[Astronaut's POV on Mars]`
- `[A marble rolling fast on a chain reaction style track, continuous smooth shot]`
- `[word by word, one word on the screen at a time: did, you, know...]`
