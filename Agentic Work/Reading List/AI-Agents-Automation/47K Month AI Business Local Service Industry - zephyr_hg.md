## Key Points
- Vertical AI implementation business model: $40-50K/month
- Target: $1M-$10M revenue service businesses with 5-50 employees
- Not SaaS or agency - more like fractional AI CTO

### Revenue Math
- 8 clients × $4,500/month = $36K recurring
- 3 implementation projects/quarter × $6K = $6K/month
- $5K/month from referrals and one-offs
- Total: ~$47K/month with 2 operators

### Top 5 Verticals
1. Real estate offices (200K+ in US)
2. Law firms (document review, intake)
3. Dental practices (patient comms, scheduling)
4. Accounting firms (tax season, bookkeeping)
5. Insurance agencies (quoting, customer service)

### The Offer
- Part 1: Build phase - $5K-$8K flat (2-4 weeks)
- Part 2: Retainer - $3K-$6K/month (maintenance, training, ROI reporting)
