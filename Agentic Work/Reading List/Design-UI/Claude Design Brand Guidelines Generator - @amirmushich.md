## Key Points

- **Problem**: Claude Design (AI design tools) need brand guidelines to produce consistent output
- **Solution**: Claude Project that generates complete brandbooks via Q&A flow
- **Output format**: HTML/PDF brandbooks with full brand platform + guidelines
- **Target audience**: SMB, solo-businesses, creators — people who can't afford brand agencies
- **Flow**: Questions → 2-3 brand directions → final HTML brand guidelines

### Why This Matters

This addresses a real gap: AI design tools generate one-off assets, but brand consistency requires guidelines (colors, typography, voice, spacing rules). A Claude Project that generates brandbooks from a Q&A could bootstrap brand identity for small teams who'd otherwise skip this step.
