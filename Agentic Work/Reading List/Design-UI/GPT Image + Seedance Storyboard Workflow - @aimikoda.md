## Key Points
- Combines GPT Image 2 with Seedance 2.0 for AI video generation
- Uses IPA (Individual Proprioception Adapter) + FACS (Facial Action Coding System) for performance capture
- Color-coded storyboard annotations to prevent model confusion:
  - Different colors separate body movement, camera motion, framing/lighting directions from environment and character drawings
- Full prompts shared in thread replies

## Technique Notes
- When mixing multiple types of instructions in storyboards, color-coding helps the model distinguish between:
  - Movement/choreography directions
  - Camera motion instructions
  - Framing and lighting guidance
  - Environment descriptions
  - Character specifications
