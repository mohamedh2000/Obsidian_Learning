# Details That Make Interfaces Feel Better - @jakubkrehel

> Jakub Krehel shares an article full of small UI details that compound into interfaces that feel more polished and intentional.

## Key Points

- The linked piece covers practical polish moves like `text-wrap: balance`, concentric border-radius math, font smoothing, and tabular numerals.
- It also explains why interaction design benefits from interruptible transitions, staggered enter animations, softer exit motion, and optical alignment.
- The throughline is that great interfaces usually emerge from many subtle layout, motion, and typography decisions rather than one dramatic visual trick.
