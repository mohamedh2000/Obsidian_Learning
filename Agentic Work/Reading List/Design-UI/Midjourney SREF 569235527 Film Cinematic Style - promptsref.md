## Style Reference

`--sref 569235527 --v 6.1 --sv4`

## Art Style Type

Blends cinematic photography, documentary realism, and retro-nostalgic aesthetics. Distinct film-like quality resembling vintage film cameras with high color saturation and natural lighting. Evokes late 70s to 90s cinema and magazine photography.

## Style Characteristics

- High contrast with vibrant color saturation
- Fine textural details with dramatic lighting
- Slight grain and film-like quality
- Raw, emotionally intense atmosphere
- Realistic skin, water droplets, and environmental textures

## Style-Enhancing Keywords

- **Film Stock**: Enhances nostalgic film quality, warmth, and grain texture
- **Natural Lighting**: Amplifies authenticity and dramatic light quality
- **Emotional Close-up**: Intensifies emotional expression and impact

## Recommended Applications

- Fashion photography
- Music album covers
- Movie promotional posters
- Brand image advertising
- Personal narrative visual storytelling
- Youth culture projects
- Nostalgic yet contemporary creative work
