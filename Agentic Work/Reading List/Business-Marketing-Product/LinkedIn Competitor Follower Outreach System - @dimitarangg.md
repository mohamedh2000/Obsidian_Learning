## Key Points
- **Competitor follower targeting**: Scrape followers from competitor LinkedIn accounts — these are pre-qualified leads already interested in the solution category
- **7-step automation pipeline**: Identify competitor → Scrape followers → Filter for ICP → Enrich emails → Segment by use case → Track activity → Send sequences
- **6x better reply rates claimed**: 8-12% reply rate on competitor followers vs 1-2% on random cold outreach — audience is pre-qualified
- **Full automation stack**: Phantombuster (scraping) → Apollo/Hunter (enrichment) → Zapier (orchestration) → Cold email tool (execution)

### The System Architecture
```
[Competitor with 10K+ followers]
         │
         ▼
[Scrape: Phantombuster/Apify/LinkedHelper]
         │
         ▼
[Filter ICP: Job titles, company size, industry]
         │
         ▼
[Enrich: Apollo/Hunter/Snov for emails]
         │
         ▼
[Segment: Startup vs Enterprise, Role-based angles]
         │
         ▼
[Monitor: Track complaints/alt-seeking behavior]
         │
         ▼
[Sequence: Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 8 → Day 12]
```

### Outreach Sequence
1. **Day 1 - Initial**: "Noticed you follow [competitor]... we built [product] to fix [pain point]"
2. **Day 4 - Case Study**: "Helped [customer] switch from [competitor] and [result]"
3. **Day 8 - Comparison**: "Made a detailed [competitor] vs [you] comparison"
4. **Day 12 - Final**: "Last email — still with [competitor] or open to exploring?"

### Objection Handling Templates
- "We're happy with [competitor]" → "Most of our customers were too, until they saw [specific thing]"
- "Switching is too much work" → "We handle migration, 48-hour turnaround"
- "Locked into contract" → "We'll cover your remaining contract costs"

### Differentiation Angles
- **Pricing**: Lower cost, same value
- **Features**: Unique capability competitors lack
- **Support**: Faster response time, dedicated channel
- **Integration**: Works with tools competitors don't support

### Ethical/Legal Notes
- Followers are public data — scraping is legal
- Email enrichment uses publicly available info
- Aggressive but within CAN-SPAM/GDPR when done properly
- Works best when you genuinely have a better product
