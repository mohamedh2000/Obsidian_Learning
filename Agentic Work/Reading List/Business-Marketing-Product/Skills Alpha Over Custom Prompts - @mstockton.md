# Skills Alpha Over Custom Prompts - @mstockton

> An interesting aspect of these models and foundation model companies: Their internal teams know a ton about how to best use these models. They are publishing things (e.g. skills) that let you essentially leverage that knowledge for free. You should never really 'hand craft' a context at this point.
