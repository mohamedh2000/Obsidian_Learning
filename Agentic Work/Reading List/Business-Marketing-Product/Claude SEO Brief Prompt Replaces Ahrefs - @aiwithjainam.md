### The 8-Phase SEO Brief Framework

The prompt systematically covers what agencies charge $200-500 to produce:

1. **SERP Analysis** — Analyze format, word count, headers, site types, freshness, and gaps in top 10
2. **Search Intent** — Map searcher goals (learn/buy/solve/find) and extract PAA/related searches
3. **Content Structure** — Generate H1, outline with H2/H3s, section word counts, internal/external link opportunities
4. **Keyword Optimization** — Target density, placement rules, 15-20 LSI keywords, 5-10 long-tail variations
5. **Content Differentiation** — Unique angle, original data, better examples, interactive elements
6. **On-Page SEO** — Meta title/description options, URL slug, image requirements, schema markup
7. **Content Requirements** — Word count, reading level, tone, expertise signals, internal/external links
8. **Success Metrics** — Target position, expected traffic, conversion goals, competitive analysis, ranking timeline
