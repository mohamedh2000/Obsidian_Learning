### AI Search Optimization Principles
Based on 2025-2026 patterns, AI search visibility requires:
1. **Clear, structured answers**: Content that directly answers questions gets cited in AI summaries
2. **Authoritative sourcing**: E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
3. **Semantic markup**: Schema.org, FAQ markup, HowTo structured data
4. **Conversational content**: Content written for how people ask questions, not just keywords
5. **Citation-worthy formatting**: Lists, comparisons, and definitive statements AI can extract

### Why This Matters
Traditional SEO optimized for click-through from search results. AI search optimization aims for:
- Being cited as a source in AI responses
- Appearing in AI Overviews/featured snippets
- Having content used for training data (brand visibility even without direct clicks)

### Strategic Implication
Businesses need content strategies that serve both algorithmic search AND LLM citation mechanics — these are increasingly overlapping but not identical.
