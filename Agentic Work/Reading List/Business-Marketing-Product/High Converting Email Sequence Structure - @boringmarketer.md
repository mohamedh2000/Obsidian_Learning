### Email Sequence Architecture (Typical High-Converting Pattern)
1. **Welcome/Hook Email**: Immediate value delivery, set expectations
2. **Pain Point Agitation**: Remind reader why they signed up, amplify the problem
3. **Social Proof**: Case studies, testimonials, results
4. **Objection Handling**: Address common concerns preemptively
5. **Offer Introduction**: Present the solution with clear CTA
6. **Urgency/Scarcity**: Time-limited or quantity-limited incentive
7. **Last Chance**: Final reminder before closing the window

### AI Integration Angle
"Give it to AI" suggests:

- Reusable templates across multiple campaigns
- Faster iteration on sequence variations
