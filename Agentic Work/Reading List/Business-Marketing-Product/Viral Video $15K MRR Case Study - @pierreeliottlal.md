### Key Insight: "Content is not branding. Content is distribution."
- **Branding mindset**: Content builds awareness over time, ROI is vague
- **Distribution mindset**: Content is a customer acquisition channel with measurable CAC
- **Implication**: Treat video production as direct response marketing, not brand fluff

### Why Video Works for SaaS
1. **Demo-able products**: Show, don't tell — video lets you demonstrate value
2. **Algorithm favoritism**: Platforms push video harder than text/images
3. **Repurpose potential**: One video → clips, GIFs, screenshots, quotes
4. **Trust building**: Face/voice creates parasocial connection

### Metrics That Matter (from this case)
| Metric | Value | Insight |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Views | 1M+ | Reach, top of funnel |
| Trials (CC) | 500+ | Qualified intent |
| Conversion | 30% | Trial-to-paid efficiency |
| MRR | $15K+ | Bottom-line impact |
| ARPU (implied) | ~$100 | 150 customers × $100 = $15K |

### Takeaway
A single piece of high-performing content can outperform months of consistent "content strategy." Asymmetric upside — worth investing in quality over quantity.
