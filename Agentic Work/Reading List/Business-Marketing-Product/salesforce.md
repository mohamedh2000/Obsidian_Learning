# Salesforce: The #1 AI CRM

> Salesforce is currently positioning itself as an AI CRM platform where apps, data, metadata, and Agentforce agents run on one governed enterprise stack.

## Key Points

- The homepage pushes Agentforce heavily, including claims around autonomous case resolution, marketing pipeline lift, and lead conversion gains.
- Its product story is a unified platform for sales, service, marketing, and industry solutions, with trust, governance, and scale presented as part of the AI value proposition.
- Beyond product messaging, the site funnels visitors into starter suites, industry-specific solutions, AI training, partner ecosystems, and broader enterprise change narratives.
