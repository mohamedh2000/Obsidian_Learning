# Setting the PM Success Metric Before Launch - @nurijanian

> A launch review is the wrong place to decide what success means — by then people can defend several charts, so the success metric has to be chosen before you build.

## Key Points

- Deciding what "success" means at the launch review is too late — competing charts can each be argued as the win.
- Activation can rise while support tickets also rise and retention stays flat, leaving no honest verdict.
- The `/success-metric` article argues PMs should lock the single success metric up front so the launch review just reads it off.
