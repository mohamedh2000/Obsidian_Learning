## Key Points

- **Traditional model**: 1 marketer handles 2-3 clients, 40-50% margin, low switching cost, human does everything.
- **Forward-deployed model** (Palantir-style): 1 marketer handles 8-12 clients, 70-80% margin, very high switching cost, human operates AI systems.
- **SEO agent**: Cross-references GSC with GA4, finds declining pages, drafts optimized updates. Test: 117 meta rewrites, 938 pages noindexed → CTR up 26%, position improved 7 spots.
- **Outbound agent**: Manages 39 sending accounts, ~2000 emails/day across 6000 leads. System diagnoses reply rate drops automatically.
- **Content agent**: Monitors competitors, scores topics by breakout probability.
