### Launch Strategy
1. **Stagger submissions**: Don't submit to all 39 on the same day
2. **Start with ProductHunt**: Time your PH launch, then cascade to others
3. **Tailor descriptions**: Each directory has different audiences — customize copy
4. **Track referrals**: Use UTM parameters to measure which directories drive traffic
5. **Update over time**: Many directories allow feature updates post-launch

### Why Directories Still Work
- **SEO juice**: Backlinks from established domains improve your search ranking
- **Evergreen discovery**: Users browse these directories for months/years
- **Credibility signal**: Being listed = social proof for visitors
- **Low effort/high upside**: 15 minutes per submission, potentially hundreds of visitors
