# When Autoresearch Fits a Problem - @ryancarson

> Ryan Carson distills a simple test for whether autoresearch is worth applying: you want fast feedback, clear metrics, and cheap experiments.

## Key Points

- The post is a quote-post on Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch work and reframes it as a product or research selection heuristic.
- Carson's three criteria make the idea more portable: the loop works best when results come back quickly, performance is measurable, and iteration cost is low.
- That makes the tweet useful less as a technical recipe and more as a filter for deciding where autonomous improvement loops make sense.
