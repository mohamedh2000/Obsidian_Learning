# 14 More Lessons from 14 Years at Google

> Addy Osmani reflects on the systems around engineering, arguing that trust, decision velocity, reliability, and coordination design matter as much as individual craft.

## Key Points

- The essay shifts from individual engineering advice to team-level lessons about picking the right problems, making meetings decision-oriented, and converting vague intent into concrete next steps.
- It treats reliability, observability, small PRs, and migration ownership as first-class operational disciplines rather than secondary maintenance work.
- A recurring theme is leverage through better systems: reduce coordination drag, avoid hero bottlenecks, and use judgment and trust as differentiators in an AI-accelerated environment.
