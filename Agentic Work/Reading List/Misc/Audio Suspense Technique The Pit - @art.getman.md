# Audio Suspense Technique - The Pit

> Explains the "pit" technique in sound design: suddenly removing almost all sound before a key moment to build maximum tension.

## Key Points

- The "pit" technique involves sharply dropping almost all sound right before a key action, phrase, or moment
- Music disappears leaving only ambient sounds: air, footsteps, breathing, or complete silence
- This creates rapid tension build-up because the brain anticipates something is about to happen
- Even ordinary everyday shots feel more impactful when suspense is built concisely with this technique
- Works on any content - thoughts, hits, clicks, or even basic transitions feel stronger
