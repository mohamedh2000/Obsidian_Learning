## Key Points

- 1-bit turn-based horror RPG set in a small Japanese town where Old Gods are waking up
- Inspired by Junji Ito, Lovecraft, Silent Hill, Siren, Paranoia Agent
- Urban legend structure: haunted schools, strange disappearances, rituals, body horror
- Every playthrough feels unique with meaningful choices affecting stamina, reason, funds, and time
- Gorgeous monochrome pixel art with customizable color palette
