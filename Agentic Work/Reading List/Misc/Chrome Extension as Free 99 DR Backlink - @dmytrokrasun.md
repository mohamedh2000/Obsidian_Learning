## Key Points
- The Chrome Web Store has a **Domain Rating (DR) of 99**, making any dofollow link from it extremely valuable for SEO
- Publishing a Chrome extension gets you a free, legitimate backlink to your domain from one of the highest-authority sites on the web
- The suggestion is to build a "tiny extension" around your SaaS product—either as a companion tool or as a replacement for standalone free tools
- This approach provides marketing value (backlink) AND product value (user utility) in a single asset

### Implementation Ideas
1. **Companion extension** — Browser toolbar for quick access to your SaaS
2. **Micro-tool** — Single-purpose utility (color picker, screenshot tool, etc.) branded to your company
3. **Free tool alternative** — Replace a popular free tool you'd otherwise link to, capture that traffic yourself

### Why It Works
- Chrome Web Store links are dofollow (pass SEO juice)
- DR 99 is near-maximum authority
- Low effort: even a minimal extension qualifies
- Double value: SEO + actual user acquisition channel
