## Key Points

- Elite paramilitary branch of the Kenya Wildlife Service
- Rangers spend months in the wild using thermal drones, GPS tracking, and trained canine units
- Intercept ivory poachers before they reach elephant herds
- Illegal ivory trade tied to global criminal networks
- Kenya's elephant population grown to over 41,000 by 2026
- Conservation now focused on climate change and habitat loss as primary threats
