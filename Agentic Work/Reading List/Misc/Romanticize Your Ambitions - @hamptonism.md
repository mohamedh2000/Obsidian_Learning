## Key Points

- The core claim: romanticizing outcomes (emotional investment in the vision) is a prerequisite for achieving them, not a distraction
- The framing is prescriptive ("you need to") rather than descriptive — this is advice, not observation
- High engagement (12K+ likes) suggests this resonates broadly, hitting a psychological truth people recognize but don't articulate
- Contrasts with "detached execution" philosophies — this advocates for emotional attachment to outcomes as motivational fuel

### Psychological Mechanism

Romanticizing goals creates emotional stakes that sustain effort through difficulty. The vision becomes vivid enough to compete with present-moment discomfort. This is visualization taken to an emotional rather than purely cognitive level.
