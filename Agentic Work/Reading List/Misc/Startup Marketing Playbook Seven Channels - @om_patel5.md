## Key Points
- **Reddit marketing**: Post as a builder sharing your journey, not selling. Share actual metrics (revenue, users, failures). Authentic posts perform 10x better. Mention product name naturally; interested users will search themselves
- **Slack/Discord communities**: Join 8-10 niche communities, help out, answer questions. Slide into DMs offering genuine help when someone posts a problem, then offer your product as the solution. Converted 5x better than public posts
- **Build in public on X**: Daily updates about progress. Grew from 0 to 9K followers. Several customers discovered through viral posts about failed ideas. Consistency > perfection
- **Cold email technique**: Find emails via Google dork: `site:"socialmedia.com" "keyword" "gmail.com"`. First email asks about the problem, second provides solution. 15% response rate, 40+ calls → 20 paying customers

### Additional Channels
- **Competitor comparison content**: Hire writers to create "[Competitor] alternative" posts targeting high-intent searches. 5x conversion rate vs regular content
- **Medium writer sponsorships**: Pay $50-$200 per article depending on following. Target any niche with high ROI. Brought 50+ customers and helped SEO
- **Show your face**: Engagement 10x higher when people see you're human, not a logo. Trust compounds

### Key Takeaways
Don't hide behind a logo. Talk to users (and listen). Keep posting even if nobody's reacting. Be open to feedback.
