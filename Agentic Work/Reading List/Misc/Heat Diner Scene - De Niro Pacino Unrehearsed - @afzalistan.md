## Key Points

- Heat (1995) was the first time De Niro and Pacino shared the screen together in the same scene
- De Niro insisted the actors retain a sense of unfamiliarity — no practice rehearsals
- Director Michael Mann agreed, creating the extraordinary tension and authenticity audiences feel
- The sequence lasts only six minutes, yet considered one of the most iconic acting duels in cinema history
- Shot at Kate Mantilini's restaurant in Beverly Hills, around 1 a.m.
- Three cameras running simultaneously on raw stock
- They only appear face-to-face twice in the entire film: the diner scene and the climax
- Heat 2 is in production starring Christian Bale and Leonardo DiCaprio
