## Key Points

- Javi describes his infrastructure journey: VPS → cloud services → back to VPS
- He admits he was "brainwashed with this convenience thing" — the serverless marketing pitch
- Says he had to "sweat blood and tears" to migrate back to his own servers
- Claims Vercel and similar platforms are overpriced by 10x-100x compared to VPS
- The lesson: viral projects expose serverless pricing models — one viral project is all it takes to see the cost difference

### The Migration Tax

Moving from serverless back to VPS requires re-learning infrastructure skills: SSH, nginx, systemd, SSL certificates, etc. The "convenience" of serverless becomes a lock-in mechanism. Javi's experience suggests the migration tax is worth paying for projects with any viral potential.
