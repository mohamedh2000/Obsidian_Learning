## Key Points

- Fritz Thaulow (1847-1906) was a Norwegian painter
- Known as "the magician of water surfaces" for his transparent, realistic water depictions
- Part of the naturalist/impressionist tradition in Scandinavian art
- Works characterized by luminous, lifelike rendering of rivers and streams
