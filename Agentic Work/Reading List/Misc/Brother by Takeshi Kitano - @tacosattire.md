## Key Points

- Brother (2000) directed by Takeshi Kitano carried quiet discipline in every frame
- Yohji Yamamoto designed the costumes with restraint—loose black suits, wide shoulders
- Wardrobe philosophy: presence comes from silence rather than display
- The clothing was never meant to stand out, but to sit inside the story
- Commenters praise the film's visual style and soundtrack (saxophone performance)
