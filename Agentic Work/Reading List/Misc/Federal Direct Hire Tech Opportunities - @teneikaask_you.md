## Key Points

- Federal government expanding Direct Hire Authority (DHA) for tech positions ahead of new fiscal year
- Positions are fully REMOTE — high-demand for government jobs with location flexibility
- DHA bypasses traditional competitive hiring, enabling faster placement for qualified candidates
- Budget announcements signal increased federal investment in technology workforce
- Jobs close quickly — time-sensitive opportunities requiring fast application

### Why Direct Hire Matters

Direct Hire Authority allows agencies to appoint candidates directly without rating/ranking or veterans' preference procedures when there's a severe shortage or critical need. For tech roles, this means:
- Faster hiring timeline (weeks vs. months)
- Competitive private-sector salaries in many cases
- Full federal benefits including FERS retirement and health coverage
