## Key Points
- Blue tone creates cool and distant atmosphere
- Reflects Neil's personality and lifestyle as professional thief
- Blue also seen in other indoor/night scenes for continuity
- Emphasizes film's noir elements
- Blue symbolizes calmness but also isolation and emptiness
- Highlights Neil's inability to form meaningful connections
