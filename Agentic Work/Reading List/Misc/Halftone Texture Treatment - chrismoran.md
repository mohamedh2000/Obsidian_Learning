## Key Points

- Technique to add halftone dots similar to old newspaper printing
- Explains why halftones give that "alive" printed feel vs digital pixels
- Same reason film looks organic: circular grain vs square pixels
- Can use built-in halftone effect in After Effects as alternative
- Similar to the moiré pattern effect
