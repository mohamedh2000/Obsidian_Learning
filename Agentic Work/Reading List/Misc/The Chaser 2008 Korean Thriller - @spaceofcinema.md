## Key Points

- The Chaser (2008) is inspired by real abduction cases including the crimes of Yoo Young-chul
- Director Na Hong-jin focused on the survival instinct of victims fighting until the very end
- Yoo confessed to murdering 21 people between 2003-2004; sentenced to death in 2004
- South Korea has not carried out an execution since 1997
- Highly praised for insane tension building
