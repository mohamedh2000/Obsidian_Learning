> No-plugin dither/halftone effect tutorial using CC Tiler + Card Dance built into AE.

## Key Points

- Create a Shape Layer (dot/circle) and pre-comp with CC Tiler
- Add Card Dance to an Adjustment Layer over your footage
- Set Gradient Layer 1 to footage, X & Y Intensity to 1
- Adjust Rows & Columns to taste for final effect
