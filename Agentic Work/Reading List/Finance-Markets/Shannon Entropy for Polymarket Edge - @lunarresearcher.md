## Key Points

- Uses Shannon Entropy (H = -sum p_i log2 p_i) to identify mispriced Polymarket contracts -- sweet spot is markets between 25c-40c with high entropy but low resolution
- Entropy efficiency ratio R = RES/U identifies "asleep" markets (R < 0.3) where crowd hasn't processed available information
- KL divergence between personal model and market odds quantifies the information edge; Kelly criterion sizes the bet
- Example trade: Fed meeting at 35c, R=0.18, model said 58%, sized at quarter-Kelly ~9% of bankroll, resolved YES for +$5,850
